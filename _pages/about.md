I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Finance and Risk Engineering](https://engineering.nyu.edu/academics/departments/finance-and-risk-engineering) at New York University, advised by Professor [Nizar Touzi](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/nizar-touzi). My research focuses on stochastic control and backward stochastic differential equations (BSDEs), with applications to economics and finance.

Previously, I received my ingénieur degree (M.S. level) in Applied Mathematics from [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) and my B.S. in Mathematics from the Chu Kochen Honors College at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/).
